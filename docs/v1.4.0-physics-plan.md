# nig-drives v1.4.0 — Physics overhaul plan ("Mario Kart → Forza 4")

Status: plan only. Baseline: v1.3.6 (`dfb7294`). No code changed.
Answer to Nigel's question: this is a **new minor version, v1.4.0**. It is delivered in small steps (M0–M9 below). Each step can ship as a v1.4.0-preX build.

**IP rule:** the BeamNG mods are used only as a source of *numbers* (torque curves, ratios, masses, brake torques, tire sizes). No meshes, textures, sounds, jbeam, Lua or JS from any mod go into the game. The Model 3 body is a new procedural mesh, built the same way as `buildGolf()`.

## (a) Why it feels arcade today (v1.3.6)

Everything below is in `src/game/vehicles/Vehicle.ts` → `Vehicle.update()` (L352–615) unless noted.

1. **The engine is not an engine.** Drive force is `throttle * spec.accel * band * gearTorque * (1 - spdFrac^powerFadeExp)` (L452–458). Torque is a function of *road speed as a fraction of `maxSpeed`*, not of RPM. `engineRpm` comes from `Transmission.rpmFromSpeed()` and is only used for the HUD and sound. Gears only scale force through `Transmission.torqueMul()`, which is clamped to 0.62–2.5 (Transmission.ts ~L104). That is why every gear pulls about the same and nothing "comes on boost".
2. **Every car is rear-wheel drive.** When accelerating, all of the force goes to the rear axle: `if (Flong >= 0) FxRear = Flong` (L528–533). The traction cap is `maxFxRear` as well (L498). A FWD Golf can never understeer on power, and lifting off the throttle does nothing to the balance.
3. **There are no tire slip states.** Lateral force is `-C * tan(clamp(alpha, ±0.6))` with a made-up cornering stiffness (`9.5`/`10.5 * mu * m g`, L519–523). There is no slip ratio, no wheel spin state and no wheel inertia. `combineGrip()` (L714) just scales forces back onto an ellipse. Grip has no peak followed by a fall-off, so slides feel like "ice or rails".
4. **Weight transfer is approximate.** Longitudinal transfer uses a finite difference of `vz` (`axApprox`, L370), clamped to ±0.28. There is **no lateral transfer** (no per-wheel loads). The CG distances `a`/`b` are computed from the *dynamic* load fractions (`wheelbase*wRear`, L516–517), so the CG moves when you brake.
5. **Yaw is held on rails.** Artificial yaw damping `exp(-(1.8+1.2mu)…)` (L574–575), plus hard clamps: `yawRate ≤ 3.5`, `|vx| ≤ 0.55*maxSpeed`, `vz ≤ 1.05*maxSpeed` (L593–600). The car cannot really rotate, slide or spin. This is the "kart" feel.
6. **Steering is digital.** `input.left/right` → ±`steerLock` (L363–368). There is no analog input, no gamepad (`src/game/input/Input.ts` only has keyboard booleans, L74–79), no self-aligning torque, and no speed-sensitive ratio beyond a rate tweak.
7. **ABS and TCS are just caps.** Total force is clipped to `0.9*mu*Fz` and a flag is raised (L494–512). Nothing modulates wheel slip, and nothing can be toggled.
8. **Variable timestep.** `Game.frame()` calls `vehicle.update(dt)` once per rendered frame with `dt = min(0.05, rawDt)` (Game.ts L394, L422). The handling changes with frame rate, and a stiff tire model would go unstable at 30 fps.
9. **Faked extras.** Boost is a smoothstep of the fake RPM (L442–445). Surface noise is `Math.random()` lateral jitter (L586–589). Aero drag is one constant shared by all cars (`0.00045*v|v|`, L481). There is no downforce.
10. **One gearbox for every car.** `RATIOS_5/RATIOS_6`, `REVERSE_RATIO = 3.6` and `FINAL_DRIVE = 3.65` are module constants in `Transmission.ts` (L16–20), shared by Golfs *and* buses.

What already exists and is worth keeping: auto/H-pattern/sequential modes and the clutch in `Transmission`; per-surface `grip`/`noise` plus wet/snow retention in `src/game/map/RoadSurface.ts`; grade gravity, `spawnHold`, Park; 4-wheel ground contact in `Game.sampleWheelContacts()`/`applyVertical()` (Game.ts L511–565); water drag; `blockAlong()` walls.

## (b) Target model (Forza 4-class, single rigid body + 4 wheels)

New folder `src/game/physics/`. Plain TS, no allocation in the hot loop, deterministic.

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| `PhysicsSpec.ts` | Data-only types: `ChassisSpec`, `TireSpec`, `EngineSpec \| MotorSpec`, `GearboxSpec`, `DiffSpec`, `BrakeSpec`, `AeroSpec`, `SuspensionSpec`, `AssistDefaults`. |
| `Tire.ts` | Brush/"Pacejka-lite" magic formula `F = D sin(C atan(B s − E(B s − atan B s)))` for slip ratio κ and slip angle α. **Friction circle** via combined-slip normalisation (σ = √(σx²+σy²)). Load sensitivity μ(Fz) = μ0·(1 − k·(Fz/Fz0 − 1)). Relaxation length (first-order lag on α), so low-speed jitter is damped *physically*, not by clamps. Pneumatic trail → self-aligning torque Mz. |
| `Wheel.ts` | Per-wheel spin state ω, inertia, drive/brake torque in, tire force out. Low-speed blend to a kinematic κ below ~1.5 m/s, which avoids the classic divide-by-zero. |
| `Suspension.ts` | Per-corner spring/damper on a vertical travel (extends the existing `sampleWheelContacts`). Gives per-wheel Fz = static + **longitudinal transfer** (m·ax·h/L) + **lateral transfer** split by roll stiffness (front/rear ARB ratio). Body roll and pitch angles for visuals and the "Feel the bumps" input. |
| `Engine.ts` | RPM state with inertia. Torque from a `[rpm, Nm]` table × throttle. Friction/engine braking (`friction + dynamicFriction*ω`). Idle controller. Rev limiter. Turbo: spool lag on boost pressure, torque multiplier from a boost table (TDI). |
| `Motor.ts` (EV) | No gears. Torque `T(ω) = min(T_max, P_max/ω)` from a table. **Instant response** (≈20–40 ms lag). **Regen**: one-pedal off-throttle regen level plus blended brake regen, limited by power and by a minimum speed. SoC drain (optional HUD). Front/rear motors for AWD. |
| `Clutch.ts` + `Gearbox.ts` | Friction clutch with torque capacity and slip (enables launches, stalls (optional), engine braking). Per-car ratios + final drive. Auto mode: shift on RPM/throttle maps (use mod-style `lowShiftUp/Down` thresholds), shift time 0.25–0.4 s with torque cut. Manual: sequential/H, rev-match off/on. |
| `Differential.ts` | Open (FWD Golf), LSD (preload + power/coast lock coefs), locked/welded. AWD centre split (Model 3 = two independent motors, so no centre diff). |
| `Brakes.ts` | Max torque per wheel, front/rear bias, handbrake on the rear. **ABS** = slip-target controller (target κ≈−0.12, 15 Hz pulse). |
| `Steering.ts` | Analog input, **speed-sensitive ratio** (full lock ≤ 20 km/h → ~35 % at 150 km/h), steer rate limit, optional countersteer assist. **Self-aligning feel**: front Mz feeds back into the steering-return rate, so keyboard players feel the wheel "go light" at the limit. Gamepad rumble scales with Mz drop and slip. |
| `Aero.ts` | Drag ½ρ·CdA·v² and rolling resistance Crr·Fz per wheel (replaces the constant). Downforce ½ρ·ClA·v² split front/rear. Road cars get ≈0. Kept for tuned/future cars. |
| `Assists.ts` | **TCS** (cut torque when driven κ > target), **ABS**, **STM/stability** (yaw-rate error vs. bicycle-model reference → brake one wheel / cut torque; mod ESC uses `slipAngleThreshold 0.08`, `yawAVThreshold 0.4` as reference tuning). Plus Forza-style toggles: ABS on/off, TCS on/off, STM on/off, Shifting auto/manual/manual+clutch, Steering normal/simulation, Braking line (later). |
| `Surface.ts` | Map `RoadSurface` kinds to tire params: μ scale *and* curve shape (loose surfaces = lower peak, flatter fall-off, higher rolling resistance). Asphalt 1.0, concrete 0.98, cobbles 0.78, gravel 0.65, dirt 0.55, grass 0.45, sand 0.40 (from the existing table). Wet/snow come from the existing `wetRetain`/`snowRetain`. Add a light **wet puddle** factor later. |
| `VehicleSim.ts` | Integrates the chassis (x, z, yaw, vx, vz, r + roll/pitch) with semi-implicit Euler at a **fixed 120 Hz** (optionally 240 Hz for tires only). |

Integration: `Vehicle.update()` stays the public entry point, so `Game.ts`, `HUD`, `ChaseCamera` and `EngineSound` keep working. Inside, it calls `VehicleSim.step()` N times from an accumulator. The existing getters (`getSpeedKmh`, `getEngineRpmNorm`, `getGearLabel`, `getAssistFlags`, `boost`, `coolant`) are fed from real state.

**Sanity notes (use the mod as a reference, then calibrate to the real ALH):**
- The mod's *base* curve already peaks at 250 Nm, before turbo boost is added. The real ALH is about **90 PS / 66 kW @ 4000 rpm and 210 Nm @ ~1900 rpm** (VW public figures; verify). Plan: keep the **mod curve shape** (diesel hump, fade after 4000) and scale it so the peak is 210 Nm with boost included. Peak arrives at 1900–2500 rpm.
- The mod's 7M ratios (5th = 0.49 with 3.89 FD) give ~300 km/h at the limiter, which is not plausible. The repo **already** has the real 02J-style ratios `3.78/2.12/1.36/0.97/0.76` (`Transmission.ts` RATIOS_5). Use those with **FD ≈ 3.39** (ALH 02J, verify): 4500 rpm in 5th ≈ 206 km/h gearing, then drag-limited to ~180 km/h like the real car. The mod's 3.89 FD is kept as a "short FD" tune option.
- The mod mass of 1210 kg is light for a 5-door TDI. The repo uses 1340 kg. Proposal: **1270 kg** curb + 75 kg driver; front weight ~61 % (diesel nose); cgHeight 0.52 m (repo); wheelbase 2.51 m (real Mk4; the repo's 2.62 m is the long-roof figure). Track 1.51/1.49 m.
- Derived for the sim: CdA ≈ 0.31×2.02 = 0.63 m²; Crr 0.012; steering lock ~35° wheel; tire μ0 ≈ 1.0 (economy tire), peak κ ≈ 0.10, peak α ≈ 7°.

Alternative Golfs already in the menu (`golf_18carb`, `golf_20aba`, `golf_28vr6`) can take the mod's other curves as references: 1.8 I4 (`Golf4_engine`: 207 Nm @ 3000, max 7000) and 1.6 (`Golf4_engine_1.6`: 178 Nm @ 4000, max 8500). The mod's 1.6/1.8 numbers are clearly inflated, so calibrate them to real figures the same way.

## Fitting into the existing game (ADD, don't replace)

How it works today:
- **Vehicle list:** `VehicleId` union, `VehicleClass = 'golf' | 'bus'`, `vehicleClassOf()` by id prefix, `VEHICLE_SPECS`, `GOLF_ENGINE_IDS`, `BUS_VARIANT_IDS` — all in `src/game/vehicles/Vehicle.ts` (L11–200).
- **Generated vehicles:** `src/game/vehicles/VehicleFactory.ts` builds the meshes procedurally: `buildGolf()` (L140), `buildNewFlyerBus(hybrid)` (L356), helpers `makeWheel()`, `fenderArch()`, `extrudeProfile()`, `std()`, `glassMat()`. `createVehicle(id, transmission)` (L539) picks a mesh by class. Wheels are tagged through `userData.wheelPivot` / `spinMesh` and collected in `Vehicle.collectVisuals()`.
- **Menu:** `src/game/ui/StartMenu.ts` `render()` writes `.menu-card` → `.section-label` + `.choice-row` of `button.choice[data-class|data-vehicle|data-trans]` with `.selected`. `onStart(StartSelection)` passes `{vehicle, transmission, …}` to `Game` (Game.ts L27–29, L157, L199).
- **HUD:** `src/game/ui/HUD.ts`: `.hud-panel` blocks (`#hud-vehicle`, `#hud-assist`, `#hud-surface`), `.hud-interactive` with `.hud-btn` (weather R / time T), `.speedo-cluster` with `.telemetry` rows (`telem-rpm`, `rpm-fill`, gear, engine, pedals, boost, coolant) and `.speedo`. Setters: `setVehicleName`, `setSpeed`, `setAssists`, `setSurface`, …. Styles are in `src/style.css` (`.menu-card`, `.choice*`, `.hud-panel`, `.hud-btn`, `.telemetry`, `.speedo*`).
- **Consumers of spec:** `EngineSound.ts` (`characterFor(spec.id)`, `soundPitch`, `class`), `ChaseCamera.ts` (`cameraHeight/Distance`, `height`, class-based eye position), `Game.sampleWheelContacts()` (`wheelbase`, `track`).

Changes (all additive):
1. **Vehicle list** (`Vehicle.ts`): add `'model3'` to `VehicleClass`; add ids `model3_rwd`, `model3_lr_awd`; add `MODEL3_VARIANT_IDS`; update `vehicleClassOf()`. **`golf_19tdi` stays the same id** (saved selections keep working) but gets the full mod-sourced `physics` block from (c). `VehicleSpec` gains an optional `physics?: PhysicsSpec`.
2. **Generated vehicles stay drivable on the new model:** new `src/game/physics/deriveSpec.ts` → `derivePhysicsSpec(spec: VehicleSpec): PhysicsSpec`, used whenever `spec.physics` is missing (golf_18carb, golf_20aba, golf_28vr6, bus_diesel, bus_hybrid):
   - mass, wheelbase, track, cgHeight, frontBias → chassis. CG a/b come from the **static** frontBias. Iz ≈ m·(L²+W²)/12 × class factor.
   - wheel radius/width → from the same constants `buildGolf()`/`buildNewFlyerBus()` pass to `makeWheel()` (export them as `GOLF_WHEEL`, `BUS_WHEEL`).
   - Engine: peak torque T = accel·m·r / (ratio1·FD·η). The curve is synthesised from `torquePeak`, `lowEndMul` and `powerFadeExp` over idle…redline, so a TDI-flavoured spec still feels like a diesel.
   - Gearbox from `defaultForwardGears(id)` + class ratios; the bus gets its own long ratios and FD (no more sharing the Golf constants).
   - Brakes from `brakeForce`·m·r split by frontBias. Tire μ0 from `gripMu`, stiffness by class (bus = low-profile truck tire: low peak α, high load). CdA from width×height×Cd(class: 0.32 car, 0.65 bus).

## (f) Risks and performance

- **Stiff tire ODEs at low speed** → jitter or creeping. Mitigate with relaxation length, a low-speed slip blend, and a static-friction "hold" (keep the existing `spawnHold`/Park logic).
- **Keyboard players.** Real tires plus digital input means spins. Keep Forza-style steering assist and speed-sensitive steer rate on by default, with a preset "Normal".
- **Tuning time** is the biggest cost. The sim-bench plus perf targets (0–100, top speed, stop distance, skid-pad g) make it measurable. Tune one car (Golf TDI) fully before deriving the rest.
- **Legacy cars feel different** after M3. That is expected, and `derivePhysicsSpec()` keeps their character. Ship M3–M7 behind a `?physics=legacy` URL flag until M9 so Nigel can A/B.
- **Bus** (14.5 t, 6.1 m wheelbase) needs its own tire curve and roll stiffness, or it will tip or float. Optionally add a rollover guard.
- **Wall/water/teleport code** (`blockAlong`, `applyWaterDrag`, `onTileSwap`, `resetToDry`) writes `vx/vz/yawRate` directly. These must also reset the wheel ω, engine rpm and suspension state.
- **CPU:** one car × 4 wheels × 120 Hz ≈ 500 tire evals/s. That is trivial (< 0.1 ms/frame). Avoid per-step allocations (reuse vectors) to protect GC on the low-end laptop. Map streaming (Overpass/tiles) stays the main frame cost. Surface sampling stays once per frame, not per substep.
- **Data accuracy:** the mod numbers are sometimes unrealistic (Golf 7M ratios, inflated base torque, light masses, Tesla 7.94 vs real 9.03 reduction, mod Tesla mass 1505 kg vs 1830 kg). Rule: **public manufacturer figures win; mod curves give shape only.**
- **IP:** no mod files or code are copied. Only numeric references are used, and they are cited in the spec files' comments.
