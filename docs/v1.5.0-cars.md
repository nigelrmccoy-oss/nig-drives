# nig-drives 1.5.0g: first car batch (40 real cars, GT2-style mix)

Status: **selection only** (planned for build 1.5.0g, not implemented in 1.5.0a). Picked from the Gran Turismo 2 list (`~/nig-drives-logs/v150/gt2-carlist.csv`) for a spread of eras (1960s to 2000s), classes (kei to Le Mans prototype) and drivetrains.
hp and kg are the **GT2 list figures** and are placeholders: every car must go through the hand-checked spec pipeline (plan section 8.1) before it ships. No logos or badges on bodies; generic parametric bodies, about 10 hero profiles. Golf TDI, Tesla, VR6, carb and ABA cars already exist and are not repeated here. Prices are shown but charge nothing in 1.5.0 (no economy).
